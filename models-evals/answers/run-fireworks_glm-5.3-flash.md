**4. The novel idea**

The generic pitch — "AI builds your automations" — centers the artifact and quietly demotes the human to spectator. This section inverts that: the artifact stops being the product, and the product becomes *provable control*. The operator isn't replaced; they're promoted — from author of logic to owner of outcomes — while the platform's new job is manufacturing evidence for every order, run, and exception. That reframes the sale from "build faster" (a copilot race we can lose) to "trust, with receipts" (a category we can own) — which is exactly what the harness dashboard makes visible. **Slide 3 carries it** — "Orders flow down. Evidence flows up" is the thesis stated as architecture — and Slide 4 plus the live demo is where the room *verifies* it instead of believing it.
