   on all three hubs). A counter diff satisfies nothing: disjoint identity sets of equal
   cardinality pass; a miss compensated by a duplicate is invisible. AC4 (identical
   notification identities) is unprovable with what is deployed today.
2. **Counter rot spans all three hubs** (PTO projected NEW/DEC mirror rows_returned; inbox
   sent_skipped=40 vs rows_to_process=215; BP skipped=2 vs rows_to_process=2). The historical
   10 days were computed by broken definitions and are invalid for the diff report. Directional
   evidence only.
3. **Two of six behavior classes have no organic shadow data.** PTO organic traffic is 100%
   reminders (NEW=0, DEC=0 across 10-16→10-20 samples); the re-action class is structurally
   absent while the inbox lookback is blank. NEW/DEC and re-action evidence must come from
   synthetic fixtures.
4. **The impl tenant fails in a weekly Friday window** (46-71 failed jobs on 09-18, 09-25,
   10-02, 10-09, 10-16 — all Fridays, ~4h hole on 10-16, root cause Workday
   `get_wql_report` "Service unavailable"). Pressure test and cutover schedule around Fridays.
5. **The gate flip has no oracle and nobody gets paged today**: `HR Genie Monitoring Mode`
   = SHADOW and both hub Recipe-ID properties are EMPTY strings. A false-positive skip on
   the optimized path is silent by nature.
6. **Vegas restart risk**: post-cutover restart of a pre-instrumentation clone = old path,
   no pre-screen, double-processing. CORRECTED 2026-10-21 (Josh): the Vegas lanes are PRODUCT
   COMPONENTS, not demo disposables — they get the same optimization treatment (see clone
   section below) and are included in promotion manifests; the earlier "exclude the Vegas
   folder" recommendation is retired. Until the lanes carry the treatment, they stay stopped.
7. Confirmed in v3's favor: all three gates exist and are false (including
   `BP Hub Optimized Path`); rollback package 42194959 exists but restore is UNREHEARSED
